technology:
  - title: "Dual-wavelength GaAlAs diode"
    text: "The source is a gallium-aluminium-arsenide diode emitting at 980 nm and 1470 nm. The 980 nm penetrates deeply and targets haemoglobin; the 1470 nm is strongly absorbed by water, concentrating the thermal effect in a smaller volume. The ablation zone therefore stays shallow and controlled, with markedly better haemostasis than a CO₂ laser — which is what makes the device usable close to delicate structures."
  - title: "How laser lipolysis works"
    text: "The fibre is introduced under the skin, in direct contact with adipose tissue. The energy released destroys the fat cells and heats the collagen fibres of the dermis, which stimulates neocollagenesis and produces a skin-tightening effect. The melted fat is then aspirated, or left to resorb."
  - title: "Delivery by optical fibre"
    text: "Energy is carried by fibre: bare for forward action, radial for circumferential diffusion inside a vein or cavity, metal-sheathed for surface work. Diameters run from 400 to 800 µm depending on the indication."
