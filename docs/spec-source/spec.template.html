01 · The projectGNIE Aesthetics v2
Chapter one

What we are building

A premium showcase website for GNIE — distributor of professional aesthetic equipment in Tunis — replacing the current single-page site with twelve dedicated machine pages, in French and English.

The machines sell between €15,000 and €30,000. The site's job is not to explain wavelengths; it is to make a clinic owner feel that this equipment is worth its price, and to put a contact button within reach at every scroll position. One converted lead pays for this website many times over.

Who visits

Owners and managers of aesthetic clinics, medspas and beauty institutes — Tunisia first, francophone Africa second. Business decision-makers, not technicians. They buy outcomes, ROI and trust, so the site leads with results and keeps the engineering in an expandable spec table for those who want it.

The single goal

Qualified leads through exactly two channels: a WhatsApp conversation pre-filled with the machine's name, or a quote request form with that machine pre-selected. No prices anywhere — the business is quote-driven.

What this site is not

No online shop or checkout · no prices · no user accounts · no blog · no admin panel to maintain · no database to back up. Every one of those is a cost and a risk that a lead-generation brochure does not need. The architecture leaves the door open (see chapter 08) without paying for it now.

Eight answers we need from GNIE

Nothing here blocks the next phase — but every unanswered item eventually becomes visible on a page. Answers can arrive one at a time.

01 · Is the machine called EMS 16 or NEO?
The manufacturer's manual is titled "NEO use manual"; your catalogue calls it EMS 16. The site currently says EMS 16.
02 · Which brand for the Hydrafacial?
The technical brief says BEAUTICIAN/NEO, but the brochure shows PZLASER printed on the device itself, and "Hydra Star" in its testimonials. The site currently says PZLASER.
03 · Does GNIE actually distribute the HOWBODY H6?
Its brochure is co-branded by HBEC (hbec.tn) — another Tunisian distributor. All HBEC branding has been stripped from the content and even from the product photo, but the machine's presence on the site should be confirmed.
04 · Exact model name of the Électrolyse 5-en-1?
Its two-page brochure only prints "5 IN 1 Permanent". A precise commercial name would improve both the page and its search visibility.
05 · Which brands appear in the home-page brand strip?
Confirmed so far: PZLASER, VISBODY. Pending: BEAUTICIAN/NEO (see 02) and HOWBODY (see 03).
06 · What exactly does GNIE commit to after the sale?
Installation, training, local after-sales service — the precise wording appears in the reassurance line under every machine's call to action, so it must be accurate.
07 · Are these the current contact details?
Found in the catalogue: Imm Emmeraude de Tunis, Rue Mohamed Badra, Bureau A-2-8, Montplaisir, Tunis 1073 · 55 157 506 / 90 157 560 · grtarek@yahoo.fr. Opening hours still needed.
08 · Seventeen technical values and two product photos
Five brochures carry no specification table, so values such as power draw, dimensions and weight are missing. V-Shape Platinum and Électrolyse 5-en-1 also need a clean product photograph. Full list in the Phase 0 report.

05 · The machine pageGNIE Aesthetics v2
Chapter five

The machine page, section by section

One template renders all twelve machines from their content files. The order is fixed and engineered for a non-technical buyer: outcomes first, proof in the middle, engineering on demand, contact everywhere.

  1. Breadcrumb
    Accueil / Machines / {machine}. Small and quiet, but it tells search engines how the site is organised.
  2. Hero
    Category label, the machine's name in large serif type, a one-sentence value proposition, and the two buttons that matter — gold Demander un devis and outlined WhatsApp. Product image framed to the right, stacking above the text on phones.
  3. Pourquoi cette machine — three to six benefit cards
    Outcome language only: what the clinic gains, not how the electronics work. This is the section that sells.
  4. Zones & indications
    Treatment areas as gold-outlined chips — double menton, abdomen, flancs, cuisses. For the three analysis devices these become measurement capabilities instead. If a machine has none, the section disappears rather than showing an empty shell.
  5. La technologie — one to four plain-language blocks
    How it works, explained for an owner evaluating an investment. Jargon stays in the spec table below.
  6. Caractéristiques techniques — the plaque
    The signature device: specifications presented as an engraved metal plate, grouped by theme. On phones each group collapses, the first one open. Values still missing are simply not shown.
  7. Gallery
    Only appears when the machine has at least two more clean images. A quiet grid — no slideshow, no lightbox.
  8. Call to action
    Full-width band: « Intéressé par la {machine} ? », the reassurance line about installation, training and after-sales, and both buttons again. The quote button carries the machine's identity to the contact form, which pre-selects it.
  9. À découvrir également
    Two related machines, already chosen for all twelve — keeps a visitor moving through the catalogue instead of leaving.
Why this order converts

A clinic owner scanning the page gets the promise in the first screen, the reasons to believe in the second, and a contact button no matter where they stop reading. The technical detail sits low on the page where it reassures the diligent buyer without slowing down the impatient one.

06 · The other pagesGNIE Aesthetics v2
Chapter six

Catalogue, home, contact

Catalogue — /machines/

A title, a single line of introduction, then a filter bar of eight pills — Toutes plus the seven categories — above a grid of twelve cards. Each card carries the product image, its category, name, a one-line summary, one key specification, and Découvrir →.

Filtering is instant and the active filter is written into the address bar, so a filtered view can be shared: you can send a clinic a link that opens directly on the two hair-removal machines. With JavaScript disabled, all twelve simply show.

Home — /

  • Hero — GNIE's positioning headline over a strong machine visual, with Découvrir nos machines and WhatsApp.
  • Categories strip — the seven categories as tiles, each opening the catalogue already filtered.
  • Featured machines — the three flagships as large cards.
  • Pourquoi GNIE — three or four trust points: official distributor, installation and training, local after-sales, international brands.
  • Brands strip — brand names set in type rather than low-resolution logo scrapes.
  • About GNIE — a compact company block: Montplaisir, Tunis; active since 2015.
  • Final call to action — both contact channels plus the newsletter signup.
  • No sliders, no autoplay, no stock-photo carousel. The restraint is the luxury.

Contact — /contact/

Coordinates on the left — address, phone, email, WhatsApp, hours. The quote form on the right: name, establishment, phone, email, machine concerned (a dropdown of all twelve plus Autre demande, pre-selected when the visitor arrives from a machine page) and a message. It posts by email through Formspree, works even with JavaScript switched off, and a hidden decoy field absorbs spam bots. Below, the Google map loads only when scrolled to, so it never slows the page down.

Legal, privacy, 404

Standard French legal notice and privacy policy drafted for a Tunisian company — the privacy page names every third party the site touches: Formspree, Brevo, Google Maps and Vercel Analytics. The 404 page is bilingual and on-brand, offering both home pages rather than a dead end.

08 · ContentGNIE Aesthetics v2
Chapter eight

How the content works — and how you change it

Every machine is a pair of plain text files, already written. Nothing is buried in the design: components display what the files contain, and never contain sentences themselves.

src/content/machines/fr/cryolipolyse-7d-360.md   ← French version
src/content/machines/en/cryolipolyse-7d-360.md   ← English version
src/assets/machines/cryolipolyse-7d-360/         ← its images

Each file holds the name, brand, category, tagline, benefits, treatment zones, technology blocks, specification table, related machines and search text. Interface labels — menu items, button text, form fields — live in a single dictionary with a French and an English column.

What this means in practice
  • Correct a specification: edit one line in two files.
  • Swap a product photo: drop the new file in the machine's folder, change one line.
  • Add machine number thirteen: two content files and an image folder. Catalogue, filters, sitemap and related-machine links pick it up by themselves.
  • Retire a machine: delete two files.
What cannot go wrong
  • Malformed content cannot ship. The build refuses to run on a missing benefit, a category typo, or an over-long search title.
  • Missing data cannot embarrass you. Values still unknown are hidden rather than printed as placeholders.
  • A missing translation is an error, not a page that silently switches language.
  • The French and English versions cannot drift apart structurally — parity is checked.
Later, if GNIE wants to edit content themselves

A free visual editor (Decap CMS) can be layered on top of these exact files — a login page where the client edits machines in a form, with no developer involved and no rebuild of the site. This is precisely why the content lives in flat files rather than a database. Parked as a future option, costing nothing today.

09 · Leads & foundationsGNIE Aesthetics v2
Chapter nine

How a visitor becomes a lead

Channel one · WhatsApp

A floating button on every page, bottom right. On a machine page the conversation opens already written: « Bonjour, je souhaite plus d'informations sur la Cryolipolyse 7D 360°. » — so GNIE knows instantly which machine the lead is about, in the visitor's own language.

Channel two · Quote request

Every Demander un devis button lands on the contact form with that machine already selected in the dropdown. The submission arrives by email. No account, no login, no friction — and it works without JavaScript.

Supporting these: a newsletter signup in the footer for long decision cycles, and analytics counting visitors, most-viewed machines, and clicks on both channels — so after launch you can see which machines attract interest before anyone even writes.

11 · Quality & riskGNIE Aesthetics v2
Chapter eleven

What "finished" means, measurably

Performance & accessibility
  • 90+ on all four Google Lighthouse categories, on a throttled mobile connection, for the home page, the catalogue and a machine page
  • No layout shifting as the page loads
  • No horizontal scrolling at 360 pixels wide
  • Fully operable by keyboard, with visible gold focus outlines
  • Touch targets at least 44 pixels
  • Colour is never the only way meaning is conveyed
Search visibility
  • A unique title and description per page, written in the content files
  • Structured data: organisation, product (without prices), breadcrumb
  • Language pairs declared on every page, French as the default
  • XML sitemap, robots file, canonical addresses
  • A share image per page for WhatsApp, Facebook and LinkedIn previews
  • Descriptive alternative text on every content image, in both languages
